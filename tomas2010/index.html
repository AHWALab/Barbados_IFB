receptor product

By parish

Severity is graded on the depth a receptor reaches: minor from 0.10 m, significant from 0.30 m, severe from 0.70 m. Likelihood is the exceedance probability at that depth, in the bands very low under 20 percent, low 20 to 40, medium 40 to 60, high above 60. A receptor takes the worst matrix cell across its severity specific probabilities. Parish risk aggregates its receptors and is then read against the same matrix.

Every parish is red. The run scored each parish on its own window, and with 120 to 380 mm of rain in every member the impact thresholds of the matrix are passed everywhere, so all eleven parishes come out HIGH. The parish fill is kept light on purpose: the information is in the receptors. Switch on the districts layer to see the 609 enumeration districts shaded by the people at yellow or worse, counted from the receptors.
One count per receptor. The run writes one GeoPackage per parish window, and a window is the parish plus a 250 m buffer, so a receptor near a boundary is scored in up to five windows. The island totals here keep each receptor once, from the window of its own parish. The per window summaries in the run outputs add up to more.
Critical facilities. Health, education and civic facilities come from the OpenStreetMap extract of 6 April 2026. Each takes the worst warning level of the buildings within 30 m of it. 528 buildings carry the critical flag in the receptor stock; 74 of them are at yellow or worse and 14 at amber or worse in this cycle.
