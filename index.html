AHWA Laboratory, University of Iowa EWS-F project, funded by the WMO, 2026 Flood maps   Flood potential

These maps combine flood likelihood from the ensemble flood map library with potential impact severity for every building, road segment and enumeration district. The warning colour of each feature comes from the Flood Risk Matrix of the Scottish Flood Forecasting Service and the UK Flood Guidance Statement, as applied operationally in Speight et al. (2018). Green means business as usual, yellow be aware, amber be prepared, red take action with danger to life.

Per forecast cycle Saint Thomas, 17 August 2026 Three cycles: 13:00, 14:00 and 16:00 UTC. 27,836 buildings, 3,486 road segments and 99 enumeration districts scored against the matrix on every cycle. The 13:00 cycle carries the only red building of the event; 14:00 has the widest amber footprint with 36 buildings and 13 road segments. The warning standard

Likelihood \ Severity MinimalMinor SignificantSevere
High, over 60 percent
Medium, 40 to 60
Low, 20 to 40
Very low, under 20

Severity is graded on the depth a receptor reaches: minor from 0.10 m, significant from 0.30 m, severe from 0.70 m. Rows are the likelihood bands of the Flood Guidance Statement. Overall district risk aggregates its receptors and is then read against the same matrix.

Companion products for the same event: the FIM results viewer shows the likelihood grids these warnings are computed from, and the UQ flood potential viewer shows the island wide hydrological guidance that decides whether a FIM cycle runs at all.
