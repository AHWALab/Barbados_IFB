These maps combine flood likelihood from the ensemble flood map library with potential impact severity for every building, road segment and admin unit. The warning colour of each feature comes from the Flood Risk Matrix of the Scottish Flood Forecasting Service and the UK Flood Guidance Statement, as applied operationally in Speight et al. (2018). Green means business as usual, yellow be aware, amber be prepared, red take action with danger to life.

The warning standard

High, over 60 percent
Medium, 40 to 60
Low, 20 to 40

Severity is graded on the depth a receptor reaches: minor from 0.10 m, significant from 0.30 m, severe from 0.70 m. Rows are the likelihood bands of the Flood Guidance Statement. Overall admin unit risk aggregates its receptors and is then read against the same matrix.
